Our Policies

Transparent & fair.

Deposit Policy

A 30% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking slot. This guarantees your barber's time and travel.

Cancellations

Need to reschedule? No problem — do it up to 24 hours before your appointment at no charge. Late cancellations forfeit the deposit.

Travel

Travel is included within our standard coverage area. Locations outside the zone may carry a small travel fee, confirmed at booking.
